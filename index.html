    02.05
    
        Allocation bench
        
    
        Two channels with diminishing returns. Split a fixed budget between them and find the point where the last unit spent on each buys the same response. That point, not the channel with the better average, is where a budget belongs.
        
    
        
      
      
    
        
    
    
        
    
        
    
        Method: each channel follows a Hill curve with an exponent at or below one, so each is concave and the constrained optimum is unique. The solver bisects on the shadow price until the marginal returns match. Verified against brute force before publishing.
        
    Illustrative. Invented parameters. Not a fitted model. Not employer work.
